The Damage Ledger — now computed

The forks above are specific calls. This used to be a fill-it-in method; the dmg/ model has since parsed the build (94% of affix lines) and computed the real numbers. Baseline: 33.8M boss DPS, 0.95s combo cycle (3 starters + finisher, 4 clones).

Your damage is a product of multiplier buckets — weapon × skill% × (1+Σincreased) × ∏(1+additional) × (points×amp) × crit × enemy-taken × (1−res+pen) × speed. The table below is the model nudging each bucket on your actual snapshot and reporting the DPS change — the “smallest bucket” hunt, done for you.

stop paying for
  • Armor / armor-mitigation penetration — dead. Fully converted cold hits check cold resistance only.
