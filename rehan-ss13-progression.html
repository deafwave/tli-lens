Rehan's damage is one skill: Spectral Slash's Combo Finisher. Everything else on this page is a system that multiplies that one number — so the build is grouped by damage system, not by what you buy when. Mods are portable and slates are a menu: treat every list here as “any mix,” not a shopping order. Every % figure on this page is now computed from the parsed build (dmg/ model, 95% of affix lines parsed — gear, tree, traits, memories and divinity slates with their actual rolls — boss single-target) — not eyeballed.

The Damage Ledger — now computed

The forks above are specific calls. This used to be a fill-it-in method; the dmg/ model has since parsed the build (95% of affix lines, now including hero memories and divinity slates with their actual rolls) and computed the real numbers. Baseline: 284.9M boss DPS, 0.75s combo cycle (3 starters + finisher, 4 clones) — the memories/slates were carrying ~8× the previously-modeled damage.

Your damage is a product of multiplier buckets — weapon × skill% × (1+Σincreased) × ∏(1+additional) × (points×amp) × crit × enemy-taken × (1−res+pen) × speed. The table below is the model nudging each bucket on your actual snapshot and reporting the DPS change — the “smallest bucket” hunt, done for you.
